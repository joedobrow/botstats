Appendix

How this was built

Method

Every readable channel in the RD2L EST‑WED server was pulled via the Discord API — 20,396 human messages across 26 channels, from 13 March to 4 September 2026. That window covers all of Season 38 plus the Season 39 signups and draft, so these are habits built up over a full season together, not first impressions. Bot messages excluded. Private channels the bot can't read (team rooms, admin channels) are not included, which means rosters coordinating privately look quieter here than they are.

Rosters came from the RD2L season‑39 team pages. Each of the 70 drafted players was matched to a Discord account through the handle on their RD2L profile, with fuzzy matching for legacy name#1234 handles and self‑reported junk. 69 of 70 matched. The exception is giga male (Actor), who has never posted a message in this server.

Sentiment is lexicon‑based, not a model: word lists tuned for Dota vernacular (gg, ty, lets go versus tilt, throw, report), counted per message and reported as a rate. Every number here traces back to something countable.

What this cannot tell you. A lexicon cannot hear tone. “you're trash” between friends and the same words meant seriously score identically. A high Tilt rate is a flag to look closer, not a verdict.

Volume is not virtue. Some of the quietest rosters here are veterans who simply coordinate elsewhere. Silence in #general is not evidence of anything except silence in #general.

And none of it measures Dota. Zero games of shared history exist. This is a read on temperament, not on skill — the standings will disagree with this page, and the standings will be right.
